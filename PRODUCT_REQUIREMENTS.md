# Product requirements

SiteGuard AI is a multi-company workplace safety SaaS. The supplied product vision
is a roadmap, not a claim of completed capabilities.

Milestone 1 acceptance: real email/password signup, verification, login/logout and
recovery; authenticated transactional organization onboarding; company-admin project
creation; membership-based organization switching; responsive real-data dashboard;
RLS and least-privilege grants; private storage foundation; automated security tests;
Vercel-compatible build and deployment documentation.

Deferred: invitations and team administration, inspections and photographs, hazard
assessment, corrective actions and independent verification, generated visual concepts,
PDFs, controlled company procedures, analytics, subscriptions, payments and usage limits.
No fake inspection metrics, credentials or AI responses are permitted.

The interface uses navy, teal and light surfaces with labeled forms, large touch
targets, pending states and explicit configuration/empty/error states. Safety decisions
remain with competent people; future AI output will require human review.
