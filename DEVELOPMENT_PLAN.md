# Development plan

1. Foundation: authentication, company onboarding, projects, responsive dashboard,
   migration/security tests, CI and deployment preparation (this branch).
2. Approved development deployment and hosted Supabase acceptance: configure email,
   complete end-to-end multi-user tests, review abuse controls and operational limits.
3. Invitation-based role management and company settings with audit trails.
4. Inspections, validated private photographs and persistent inspection history.
5. Real vision integration with structured validation and human review.
6. Corrective actions, evidence and independent closure verification.
7. Reports, conceptual visualizations and company safety references.
8. Entitlements, usage metering, payments, monitoring and production readiness review.

Do not enable production credentials, run remote migrations or deploy production
without the owner's approval. Keep each feature in a reviewable PR.
